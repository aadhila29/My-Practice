ABOUT CAMBRIDGE

About the University

A historic university with a global academic community.

OUR STORY

Centuries of learning and discovery

The University of Cambridge traces its origins to 1209 and has developed into a major international centre for education and research.

Its distinctive structure brings together the University, academic Faculties and Departments, and a network of Colleges.

This collegiate environment gives students access to both specialised academic communities and broader opportunities across the University.

1209

A continuing academic tradition

Cambridge has been at the forefront of academic study and research for centuries.

HOW CAMBRIDGE WORKS

A collegiate university

Students belong to both the University and a College, creating a distinctive academic and social experience.

31 Colleges

Colleges provide communities where students live, study and take part in social and cultural life.

6 Schools

Academic activity is organised across six broad Schools covering major areas of knowledge.

150+ Faculties & Departments

Specialist academic communities support teaching, scholarship and research across many disciplines.

Our Mission

Cambridge's mission is centred on contributing to society through education, learning and research at the highest international levels.

Education and research can create knowledge, encourage curiosity and contribute to society.

Our Community

Cambridge is home to students and staff from many countries and backgrounds.

Academic life is supported by libraries, laboratories, museums, sports facilities, student organisations, Colleges and many other services.
