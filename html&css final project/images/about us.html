OUR UNIVERSITY

Inspiring Minds, Creating Possibilities

The University of Cambridge is one of the world's leading centres of education, learning and research. For centuries, Cambridge has brought together talented people from around the world in an environment where knowledge, curiosity and innovation can flourish.

Our community is built on a shared passion for learning and a commitment to making a meaningful contribution to society.

From the humanities and social sciences to science, technology and medicine, Cambridge continues to encourage new ideas and discoveries that can shape the world.

OUR HERITAGE

Centuries of Learning and Discovery

Cambridge has a remarkable history of education and intellectual discovery. Its traditions provide a strong foundation for modern scholarship while its academic community continues to look towards the future.
