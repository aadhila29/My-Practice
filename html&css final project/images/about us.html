A university with centuries of history.

OUR UNIVERSITY

Where history, learning and discovery come together.

The University of Cambridge is a world-leading university located in Cambridge, England. It has a long tradition of learning, teaching and research.

The University brings together people from many different backgrounds and countries. Students, academics and researchers work together as part of a diverse academic community.

Cambridge is made up of a central University and 31 Colleges. Each College has its own community, facilities and traditions.

OUR HISTORY

More than eight centuries of learning.

The University of Cambridge dates from 1209. Over the centuries, Cambridge has developed into a major centre for education and research.

Its history is reflected in its buildings, libraries, Colleges, traditions and academic community.

CAMBRIDGE TODAY

Looking towards the future.

Cambridge continues to support teaching and research across a wide range of subjects. Its academic community works on ideas and challenges that affect people around the world.

Alongside its historic traditions, the University continues to develop new areas of research, education and collaboration.

THE COLLEGIATE SYSTEM

31 Colleges, one Cambridge community.

Colleges are an important part of life at Cambridge. They provide communities where students can live, study and take part in activities.

Each College has its own character and traditions while being part of the wider University.

A global academic community.

Cambridge attracts students and researchers from around the world. This international community brings different experiences, perspectives and ideas together.

Through teaching, research and collaboration, members of the University contribute to knowledge and society.
